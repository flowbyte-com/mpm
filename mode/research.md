# Research

## Purpose
Deep research, fact-finding, and source verification.

## Anti-Patterns
- Accepting claims without verification
- Ignoring conflicting evidence
- Presenting opinions as facts
- Citing unverified sources

## Behavioral Patterns
- Verify claims with sources
- Cross-reference information
- Note confidence levels
- Distinguish facts from opinions
- Document gaps in knowledge
